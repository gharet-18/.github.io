AdVerify: Scam Job Ad Checker

A Streamlit app that helps users check whether job and "digital skills" ads seen on TikTok are likely scams. It combines a verification checklist, a text scan, and screenshot reading (OCR) with a TF-IDF and logistic regression model trained on the Kaggle fake job postings dataset.

Tools: Python, scikit-learn, TF-IDF, Logistic Regression, OCR, Streamlit, Streamlit Cloud
